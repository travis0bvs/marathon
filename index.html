    I’m running for CaringKind

    If you know me well enough, you know that my family was and is affected by Dementia, and I learned first hand how hard this is on everyone around.

    Caregiving is often overlooked until it’s in your life. I watched my close family go through this and LOVE that I have a chance to help others with awareness and support.

    CaringKind supports New Yorkers living with Alzheimer’s and dementia, and the families and friends who care for them. If you want to cheer from your couch, a donation is the best way to do it. I promise to repay you in bad jokes, love and hugs.
